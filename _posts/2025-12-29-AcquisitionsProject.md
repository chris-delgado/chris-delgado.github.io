I took CS 8803 Computer Law during the Fall 2025 semester. In one of the projects in that class, we had to propose a hypothetical acquisition and argue why such an acquisition would make sense financially and strategically. The report I wrote analyzes a proposed acquisition of Whisker by Chewy, Inc., arguing that the deal would expand Chewy’s business into the automated pet care market while strengthening its position in the pet industry.
